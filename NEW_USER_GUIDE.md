# 🎮 Mai Troll - New User Guide

Welcome to **Mai Troll**, a live streaming and social community platform where you can broadcast, connect with others, join families, and participate in a unique virtual city experience.

---

## 📋 Quick Start (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Visit the Mai Troll website
2. Click **Sign Up** 
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Choose a unique **username** - this is your identity in Mai Troll


### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
- Add a bio to tell others about yourself

### Step 3: Explore the City
- Start by visiting the **Home** page to see live streams
- Check out the **Explore** feed to discover content

### Step 4: Start Engaging
- Watch live broadcasts and chat with others
- Follow users you enjoy
- Consider joining a **Family** (clan) for community
- Try **going live** yourself when you're ready

## 🛡️ Moderation Policy

### Human Moderators - No AI Moderation

Mai Troll is moderated by **real human moderators** - not AI. Our team of dedicated staff works around the clock to:

- Review reported content
- Enforce community guidelines
- Handle disputes and conflicts
- Maintain a safe environment

### Freedom of Speech - But To An Extent

Mai Troll values **freedom of expression**, but with reasonable limits:

**✅ You Can:**
- Express opinions and viewpoints
- Engage in debates and discussions
- Create content on various topics
- Join families and participate in family wars

**❌ You Cannot:**
- Post illegal content or activities
- Harass or bully other users
- Share personal information about others (doxxing)
- Engage in scamming or fraud
- Post explicit sexual content
- Evade bans (ban evasion)
- Abuse the gift system

### Reporting Issues

If you encounter problems:
1. Click a user Username then click Red Triangle to Report
2. Select the appropriate report category
3. Submit your report
4. Our Officers will review your case

### Categories You Can Report:
- Nudity / Sexual Content
- Doxxing / Personal Info
- Scamming / Fraud
- Chargeback / Payment Abuse
- Gift Manipulation
- Ban Evasion
- Family War Dispute
- Streamer Misconduct
- Officer Misconduct
- Appeal Case
- Copyright / Content Claim
- Mai Troll Policy Violation
